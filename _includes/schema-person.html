{% comment %} Structured data: who Blair Purvis is. jekyll-seo-tag already describes each *page*. This describes the *person*, and does the one job the plugin can't: `sameAs`. Every profile listed in `social:` and `profiles:` in _config.yml is declared here as the same human being, which is how blairpurvis.com becomes the answer to "Blair Purvis" rather than one candidate among a physiotherapist and a parked domain. The @id is a stable anchor. Book pages point their `author` at it instead of repeating the name, so a search engine reads four books by one author rather than four books by four authors who happen to share a name. Nothing here needs editing. It rebuilds itself from _config.yml. {% endcomment %} {%- assign profiles = "" | split: "" -%} {%- for s in site.social -%} {%- assign scheme = s.url | slice: 0, 7 -%} {%- if s.url and scheme != "mailto:" -%}{%- assign profiles = profiles | push: s.url -%}{%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- for p in site.profiles -%} {%- if p.url -%}{%- assign profiles = profiles | push: p.url -%}{%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%}
